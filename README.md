# Microsoft Office for Business

A free, two-session beginner workshop covering the Microsoft 365 apps used most in business settings: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, and Microsoft 365 Copilot.

**Instructor:** Dr. Carelicha Duckworth, PhD

| Session | Date | Length | Topics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session 1 | Monday, November 2, 2026 | 3 hours | Microsoft 365 and OneDrive, Word, Excel |
| Session 2 | Wednesday, November 4, 2026 | 3 hours | PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams, Copilot, Microsoft Learn badge |

## For students

Download these two files before Session 1.

- [Student Workbook](https://github.com/DrDuckworthTri-C/microsoft-office-for-business/raw/main/student/Student_Workbook.docx) (Word): six lessons with step-by-step instructions, practice activities, and self-checks
- [Expense Tracker Starter](https://github.com/DrDuckworthTri-C/microsoft-office-for-business/raw/main/student/Expense_Tracker_Starter.xlsx) (Excel): sample data for Lesson 3 if you prefer not to type it

### Before the first session

1. Create a free Microsoft account and sign in to [Microsoft 365 for the web](https://www.microsoft.com/en/microsoft-365/free-office-online-for-the-web).
2. Sign in to [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/) with the same account. You will use it in Lesson 6 to earn your badge.
3. Bring a laptop if the session is not in a computer lab.

### What you will complete

- A business memo in Word (DOCX and PDF)
- An expense tracker in Excel with totals, an average, currency formatting, sorting, and a chart
- A five-slide business presentation in PowerPoint
- An email with a OneDrive link and a Teams meeting on your calendar
- The [Microsoft Productivity Accreditation 2026](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/microsoft-productivity-accreditation-2026/) badge on Microsoft Learn
