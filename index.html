Before the first session

These three things take about ten minutes and mean you can start working the moment class begins.

  1. Create a free Microsoft account. Go to Microsoft 365 for the web and click Sign up for free. If you already have an Outlook.com, Hotmail, or Xbox account, that works too.
  2. Sign in to Microsoft Learn. Open learn.microsoft.com and sign in with the same account. You will use it in the last lesson to earn your badge.
  3. Download the workbook. Use the button above. Keep it open during class. Bring a laptop if the session is not in a computer lab.

What we cover

Session 1

Monday, November 2. Three hours with two breaks.

  • Lesson 1: Microsoft 365 and OneDriveSign in, find the apps, create folders, share a file with view or edit permission.
  • Lesson 2: WordBuild a one-page business memo from a template: styles, lists, a table, a header, spell check, export to PDF.
  • Lesson 3: ExcelBuild an expense tracker: enter data, SUM and AVERAGE, currency format, sort, and a chart.

Session 2

Wednesday, November 4. Three hours with two breaks.

  • Lesson 4: PowerPointA five-slide business presentation with a theme, a picture, and your Excel chart.
  • Lesson 5: Outlook and TeamsSend email with a OneDrive link, schedule a Teams meeting, join a meeting, post in a channel.
  • Lesson 6: Copilot and your badgeWhat Copilot does and how to prompt it, then earn the Microsoft Productivity Accreditation 2026.
